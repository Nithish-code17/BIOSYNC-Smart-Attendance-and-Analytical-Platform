# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the BIOSYNC project, regardless of background, identity, experience level, education, nationality, appearance, disability, religion, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction professionally.
- Respect privacy, especially when discussing educational and biometric systems.
- Avoid publishing personal, institutional, attendance, or credential data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal insults.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing real biometric, attendance, student, staff, phone, credential, or institutional data in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour violates this Code of Conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as when representing the project publicly.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by established open-source community conduct standards and is adapted for the privacy-sensitive nature of BIOSYNC.
